Creating long-term value in North Texas real estate.

Brute Force CRE is a privately held real estate investment firm focused on the Dallas–Fort Worth region. We combine disciplined capital allocation with an integrated operating platform to acquire, enhance and manage real assets.

The Firm

We invest with a long-term perspective, an owner's mindset and a hands-on approach to every asset in our care.

Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Texas, Brute Force CRE invests in real estate across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, one of the fastest-growing metropolitan regions in the United States.

Our investment activity is supported by a vertically integrated operating model. By bringing acquisition, renovation and asset management under one roof, we seek to enhance the quality of our assets, protect capital and generate durable value over time.

We remain flexible in how and where we deploy capital, allowing us to respond to changing market conditions and pursue opportunities as they emerge.

Our Platform

An integrated operating platform

Our in-house remodeling and construction capability is central to how we create value. Rather than relying on third-party contractors, we maintain direct oversight of property improvement, giving us a distinct operational advantage at every stage of ownership.

Execution certainty

Direct control of renovation scheduling and resources reduces dependence on outside parties and supports timely delivery of improvement programs.

Quality and standards

Work is completed to consistent internal standards, with every improvement selected to support the long-term performance of the asset.

Cost transparency

Integrated delivery provides clear visibility into project costs and supports disciplined budgeting across our holdings.

Operational insight

Hands-on construction experience informs how we evaluate opportunities, assess physical risk and plan asset enhancements.

Our Approach

Guided by enduring principles

Our approach rests on a small set of principles that guide how we invest, operate and work with our partners.

Disciplined investing

We approach every opportunity with rigorous diligence and a focus on capital preservation, investing only where we see a clear path to long-term value.

Active stewardship

We are owners and operators, not passive investors. We stay closely involved in the improvement and management of every asset we hold.

Integrity and alignment

We hold ourselves to high standards of conduct and transparency, and we build lasting relationships with the partners, lenders and communities we work with.

One integrated team, accountable for each asset from acquisition through long-term ownership.
